## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**PepTrack Pro - Your All-in-One Health Tracking Companion**

Take control of your health journey with PepTrack Pro, the ultimate app for tracking peptides, supplements, and medications. Whether you're an athlete optimizing recovery, managing your daily supplements, or tracking prescription medications, PepTrack Pro has you covered.

🔬 **PRECISION DOSAGE CALCULATOR**
Our flagship reconstitution calculator takes the guesswork out of peptide preparation:
• Select your syringe size (0.3mL, 0.5mL, 1.0mL, or custom)
• Enter your vial amount and bacteriostatic water volume
• Get precise "units to draw" calculations instantly
• Visual syringe fill animation shows exactly where to draw
• Save presets for your frequently used calculations

📅 **SMART SCHEDULING & TRACKING**
Never miss a dose with intelligent scheduling:
• Create recurring schedules (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or custom)
• Set multiple times per day (morning, afternoon, evening, bedtime)
• Track each dose as Taken, Skipped, or Delayed
• View your adherence percentage at a glance
• Week and month calendar views

📚 **COMPREHENSIVE RESEARCH DATABASE**
Access detailed information on 50+ compounds:
• 29 peptide profiles with dosage guidelines
• 20+ medications and supplements
• AI-powered research for any compound
• Add your own custom entries
• Filter by category or goal

📓 **HEALTH JOURNAL**
Track how you feel and monitor trends:
• Log weight, energy, sleep quality, and mood
• Add notes and observations
• View 7-day trend charts
• Export your data anytime

🌓 **LIGHT & DARK MODE**
Easy on your eyes with automatic theme switching based on your device settings, or choose your preferred mode.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**
PepTrack Pro is for informational and personal tracking purposes only. This app is NOT intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any peptide, supplement, or medication regimen.
