# PepTracker Pro – Support

Welcome to the official support page for **PepTracker Pro**, the research-grade peptide tracking and dosage calculator app for iOS.

## Contact & Support

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, please [open an issue](https://github.com/CoastalCrypto/Dr.-Peptide-/issues) on this repository.

You can also reach us directly at: **support@peptrackerpro.com**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What does PepTracker Pro do?**  
A: PepTracker Pro helps researchers and health enthusiasts track peptide protocols, calculate dosages based on body weight and vial concentration, and log administration schedules.

**Q: Is the information in the app medical advice?**  
A: No. PepTracker Pro is intended for informational and research purposes only. All calculations and content in the app are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any peptide protocol.

**Q: How are dosage calculations performed?**  
A: Calculations are based on standard research protocols. All formulas reference published scientific literature – see the Citations section in the app for full source details.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**  
A: Please open a GitHub Issue on this repository with a description of the problem and your device/iOS version.

## Privacy Policy

PepTracker Pro does not collect or transmit any personal health data. All data is stored locally on your device.
