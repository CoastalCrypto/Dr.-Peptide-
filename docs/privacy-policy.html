PepTrack Pro

Privacy Policy

PepTrack Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Health & Tracking Data: Peptide, supplement, and medication usage records you enter, including names, dosages, schedules, and administration routes.
  • Health Journal Entries: Weight, energy levels, sleep quality, mood ratings, pain levels, and personal notes you choose to log.
  • Calculator Presets: Reconstitution calculations and saved configurations.
  • Account Information: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise geolocation or GPS data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS data
  • Data from other apps on your device
  • Biometric data

Automatically Collected Information

  • Basic device identifiers for crash reporting and app stability
  • App usage analytics (anonymized and aggregated)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide core tracking, scheduling, and calculation features
  • Generate AI-powered health insights and research summaries (when you use these features)
  • Sync your data across devices (only if you sign in with Google)
  • Improve app performance, fix bugs, and enhance features
  • Send you dose reminders and notifications (only if enabled)

We will NEVER: sell your personal health data to third parties, use your data for advertising purposes, or share individually identifiable health information with external parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local-First Storage: By default, all your data is stored locally on your device. The app is designed to function fully offline.
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): If you sign in with Google, your data may be synced to secure cloud servers (MongoDB Atlas with encryption at rest) for backup and cross-device access.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between the app and our servers use TLS 1.2+ encryption.
  • Access Control: Your data is accessible only to you through your authenticated device or account.
  • Server Security: Our backend infrastructure uses industry-standard security practices including connection pooling, rate limiting, and secure session management.

4. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Guest Mode: Use the app without creating an account. All data stays on your device only.
  • Data Export: Export all your data at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Data Deletion: Permanently delete all your data from within the app at any time. For cloud-synced data, deletion removes data from both your device and our servers within 30 days.
  • Edit or Correct: Modify or delete any entry you have made at any time.
  • Opt-Out of Cloud Sync: You can use the app entirely in offline/guest mode.

For European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and the right to object. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

For California Users (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

PepTrack Pro integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For optional account authentication, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • AI Language Model Services: For generating health summaries and research information. Queries are processed without persistent storage of your personal health data.
  • Crash Reporting: Basic crash and error reporting to improve app stability. No personal health data is included in crash reports.

6. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud Data: Retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all data is removed within 30 days.
  • Server Logs: Basic server access logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes, then automatically deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

PepTrack Pro is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

8. Medical Disclaimer

PepTrack Pro is designed for informational and personal tracking purposes ONLY. This app is NOT a medical device and should NOT be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. The dosage calculations, research information, and suggested protocols are for educational reference only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting, changing, or stopping any peptide, supplement, or medication regimen.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date below and notify users through the app. Your continued use of PepTrack Pro after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
